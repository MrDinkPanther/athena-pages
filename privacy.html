Athena Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Athena is a personal desktop app that runs on the developer's own Windows PC. It is a private, single-user project. This policy describes what the app does with Google account data.

What the app accesses

If the developer chooses to connect a Google account, the app requests read-only permissions, and nothing more:

  • Google Calendar (calendar.readonly): the title, time and location of upcoming events.
  • Gmail (gmail.readonly): the sender, subject, date and text of email messages that match what the developer asks the assistant about.

The app cannot create, change, send or delete anything in the calendar or in Gmail.

How the data is used and stored

  • Calendar and email details are shown inside the app on the developer's PC and are used to answer the developer's own questions in the app's chat.
  • In the chat, calendar or email details are read only when the developer's message asks about the calendar or email. Only the items relevant to that question are used.
  • When the optional phone link is on, the developer can also open Calendar and Email panels in the phone's World view. Opening one reads the upcoming calendar events, or the unread email (sender, subject, date and a short preview), and shows them only in the developer's browser on that phone, over the same private, encrypted Tailscale connection. This data is not sent to an AI model provider for this purpose, is not stored by the phone page, and is read only when the developer opens the panel.
  • To produce an answer, those details are sent to the AI model provider the developer has chosen in the app: Groq or Anthropic (Claude). They are sent for that purpose only. Each provider handles the data under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Answers appear in the app's chat, and the chat history is saved on the developer's PC. It is not sent to any server operated by the developer.
  • When the developer is using the app's optional phone link, the text of the chat, including answers that contain calendar or email details, and spoken audio of the replies, is also sent to the developer's own phone. This happens only if the developer switches the phone link on. It travels over a private, encrypted connection between the developer's own devices (Tailscale), is shown only in the developer's browser on that phone, and is not sent to any other person or server. The developer can switch the phone link off, or revoke the phone's access, at any time.
  • The app stores a Google sign-in token on the PC, encrypted with the Windows data protection system. It is never sent anywhere except to Google.
  • Google data is not sold, is not shared with third parties other than the AI model provider described above, and is not used for advertising or to train generalised AI models by the developer.
  • Nobody reads the data other than the developer, apart from what the AI model provider's own systems process to produce the answer, and where required by law.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Athena's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting and deleting

Choosing Disconnect in the app revokes the app's access at Google and deletes the stored token from the PC. Access can also be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes

If the app's handling of Google data changes, this page will be updated before the change takes effect.
